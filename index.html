Liner notes, folded into the case.

The insert nobody reads until the second listen: how a seven-member group from four cities went from a debut single to a stadium in fourteen months.

  1. 2023.11.27BATTER UP is released as the debut digital single, before the group has a name people can spell. The video sets a record for a K-pop debut with 22.59 million views in a day.
  2. 2024.02.01Stuck In The Middle arrives as a pre-release, a deliberately quiet answer to a loud debut.
  3. 2024.04.01Debut. BABYMONS7ER, the first mini album, lands with SHEESH as its title track — the first of the group's top-ten entries on Korea's Circle Digital Chart.
  4. 2024.07.01FOREVER, a digital single, keeps the release cadence at one per quarter.
  5. 2024.11.01DRIP, the first full album, sells more than a million copies domestically and puts the group on the Billboard 200 at number 149.
  6. 2025.01The Hello Monsters world tour opens. It runs to September, playing 32 shows in 20 cities to around 300,000 people, and makes BABYMONSTER the fastest female act to headline KSPO Dome.
  7. 2025.05.09Rami begins an indefinite hiatus for her health. Releases continue with six members.
  8. 2025.07.01HOT SAUCE is released by six members, the first single without Rami's voice on it.
  9. 2025.10.10WE GO UP, the second mini album, arrives as the tour's first leg closes.
  10. 2026.01.03The Love Monsters fan-con tour concludes, having played to roughly 140,000 people.
  11. 2026.05.04춤 (CHOOM), the third mini album, lands with a title that is also the name of the next tour.
  12. 2026.06.08SUGAR HONEY ICE TEA is released as a digital single.
  13. 2026.06.26The CHOOM world tour opens in Seoul.

Timeline compiled from the official YG Entertainment artist pages and Wikipedia. Tour figures are the reported totals.

MONSTIEZ, and the fine print.

The fandom is called MONSTIEZ. The colours are red and black. If you are reading this section, you are already one of them, which is the fastest any fan page has ever recruited.

Side effects

  • Humming for three to five business days.
  • Replaying the bridge at 2am. There is no cure.
  • Correcting people who say “Baby Monster”. Gently. Twice.
  • Owning more than one version of the same album.
  • Sudden loyalty to a colour you had no opinion about last year.

If symptoms persist, you are a MONSTIEZ. Consult the disc.
